## 🕶️ What I'm building

**🏟️ Free-roam multiplayer VR arena** — location-based VR where players move
freely through shared physical space while staying synchronized in the virtual
one. **Live in three commercial venues**, running standalone on Quest at
**90fps** with networked physics and real-time avatar sync. Photon Fusion, no PC
tether, no fixed play-space lock.

**🌴 [Ruins of the Forgotten](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2726410/Ruins_of_the_Forgotten__A_Wild_Survival/)**
— open-world survival game, playable demo on Steam, Early Access Q3 2026.
Procedurally generated island terrain, day/night simulation, wildlife AI that
branches on time-of-day and player state, custom HLSL shaders, and the full
Steamworks pipeline through build, depot, and store submission.

**🏭 Enterprise XR training and simulation** — 15+ XR applications across Quest,
HoloLens, Vive, and Varjo at
[2 Dude Studios](https://2dudestudios.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile_readme&utm_campaign=profile&utm_content=studio_link).
